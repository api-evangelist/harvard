Harvard Library on Tuesday unveiled a redesigned website aimed at making it easier for users to discover and access its vast array of resources and services. The new site, built on the Library’s existing Drupal platform, draws on years of user research and analytics gathered since the previous version launched in 2018 . The update reflects a deeper understanding of how students, faculty, and researchers navigate the Library’s vast digital ecosystem.
