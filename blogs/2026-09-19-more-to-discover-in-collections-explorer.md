Collections Explorer , Harvard Library’s AI-powered search tool for special collections and archives, has been refreshed with a new design and millions more records to explore. Collections Explorer lets you search Harvard’s collections using everyday language. You can ask a question, describe a topic, or start with an idea rather than figuring out the exact keywords that might appear in a catalog record.
