The next generation of HOLLIS for Archival Discovery is now available for research use. HOLLIS for Archival Discovery, Harvard Library’s primary archival and special collections discovery system, has received a major upgrade. The new platform, powered by ArcLight, features enhanced search, embedded digital content, and an improved look and feel.
