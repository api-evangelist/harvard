Oct 2 , 16:08 EDT Monitoring - Following planned network maintenance, some Windows devices may need to reconnect to the Harvard Secure wireless network. To do this: Visit getonline.harvard.edu Select ‘I have a HarvardKey’ and follow the onscreen prompts For more detailed instructions, please visit Connect to Harvard Wireless If you continue to experience issues connecting to the network, contact the HUIT Service Desk at 617-495-7777 or ithelp@harvard.edu.
